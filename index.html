What this prototype leaves open

Community authority. The host and community must define who participates, how decisions are made and how dissent is handled.

Real enforcement. A production system needs identity, access controls, security review and legal/ethical approvals before handling sensitive records.

Withdrawal. Here it blocks future requests. It cannot undo a past disclosure or remove data from an already trained model.
